# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) in USD and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the components table in `docs/02-concept.md`. Item 14 is in the BOM but not modelled.

The parts total about **$299**, within the $300 concept budget with about $1 of margin. The total does **not** include safety equipment: a fire extinguisher or water drum and shovel, heat-resistant gloves, eye protection and a dust mask (about $40). Whether the budget should include them is proposed, awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).

The largest cost drivers are the water jacket ($55), the insulation blanket ($35), the temperature logger ($32) and the flue ($30). A version without the jacket, tap and tripod would cost about $212; a clay and ash render in place of the blanket would save about $25.

Drums are priced as used. Never use a drum that held fuel, solvent or pesticide, and never use galvanized pipe or drums in the hot path.
